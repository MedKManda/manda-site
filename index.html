Our model

From prototype to scale — with the right partner

We don't just consult — we build. Our model is to create advanced, validated AI prototypes and connect them with the right organizations to take them further.

01

Identify the opportunity

We work with organizations or independently to identify high-value language and knowledge problems where AI can make a meaningful difference.

02

Build the advanced prototype

We develop a fully functional, domain-specific AI prototype — not a demo, but a real product with tested functionality, designed for a clear user and use case.

03

Find the right partner or acquirer

Once validated, we look for an organization best positioned to scale the product — whether through acquisition, licensing, or a strategic partnership.
